# CampusEdge — Student Candidate Manual

## 1. Welcome to CampusEdge

CampusEdge is your campus placement intelligence and skill acceleration cockpit. It combines verifiable diagnostic assessments, transparent topic mastery tracking, and personalized 28-day preparation roadmaps to maximize your placement readiness.

---

## 2. Getting Started & Onboarding

1. **Sign In**: Navigate to `/login` using your official college email address (e.g., `student@apex.edu`).
2. **Consent & Verification**: Review and accept the statutory Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act terms.
3. **Profile Setup**: Confirm your branch code, graduation batch year, CGPA, and career target (e.g., _Software Engineer_ or _Core Engineering_).

---

## 3. The Baseline Diagnostic Assessment

### 3.1 Why Take the Diagnostic?

The diagnostic test benchmarks your conceptual strengths and weaknesses against past campus hiring standards. Completing it unlocks your **Placement Readiness Score** and generates your personalized **28-Day Preparation Roadmap**.

### 3.2 Assessment Format

- **Questions**: 45 multiple-choice questions.
- **Duration**: 60 minutes strictly enforced by the server clock.
- **Topics**: Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, and (for Software track) Computer Science Fundamentals.

### 3.3 Test Integrity Rules

- Do not switch tabs or minimize the test window; tab switches are recorded.
- Option order is randomized per candidate to prevent collaboration.
- Ensure a stable internet connection. If disconnected, reconnect promptly; the countdown timer continues running on the server.
- Questions marked for review appear in purple on the right palette.
- Solutions and verified explanations unlock immediately after final submission.

## 4. Your Placement Readiness Cockpit

Navigate to `/student/readiness` to monitor your readiness trajectory:

### 4.1 Understanding Your Score (0 – 100)

Your readiness score is a weighted composite of four deterministic indicators:

1. **Aptitude Mastery (30% – 55%)**: Bayesian mastery estimate across quantitative, logical, and verbal topics with difficulty weighting.
2. **Technical Mastery (0% – 35%)**: Data structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems, and computer networks (software track).
3. **Mock Assessment Index (20% – 30%)**: Timed, proctored test simulation scores.
4. **14-Day Practice Consistency (15%)**: Consistency score based on active daily practice habits.

### 4.2 Statutory Preparation Indicator Notice

> **Notice**: _The Placement Readiness Score is a diagnostic preparation indicator designed to measure conceptual mastery and practice consistency. It does NOT constitute a guarantee, prediction, or legal assurance of employment or recruitment selection by CampusEdge or partner institutions._

## 5. Your 28-Day Preparation Roadmap

Navigate to `/student/roadmap`:

- **4-Week Structured Sprint**: Focuses on your identified weak topics first.
- **Daily Practice Routines**: Contains targeted question counts and concept review suggestions.
- **Marking Tasks Done**: Click the checkbox on each task as you complete your daily practice.
- **Recalibration**: When you take new tests or an upcoming campus drive is announced, click **Recalibrate Roadmap** to update your sprint priorities.

---

## 6. Participating in Campus Drives & Sharing Experiences

- **Eligible Drives**: View upcoming recruiter visits at `/student/drives`. The platform displays your eligibility status based on college criteria.
- **Interview Archives**: View past interview rounds and recurring questions at `/student/companies`.
- **Anonymous Experiences**: Share your interview debriefs at `/student/experiences` to help juniors; candidate identities remain strictly confidential.
