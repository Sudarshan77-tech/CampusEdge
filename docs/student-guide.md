# CampusEdge — Student Quick Start Guide (One-Page)

## 1. Quick Onboarding

1. Visit your campus portal at `https://campusedge.internal` (or local development address).
2. Click **Sign In** and enter your official college email address (e.g. `student@apex.edu`).
3. Complete the digital **DPDP Act Consent Agreement** on your first login.
4. Verify your branch, graduation year, and target career track on your profile.

---

## 2. Taking the Diagnostic Test

- **When**: Upon initial onboarding.
- **Duration**: 60 Minutes (autoritative server countdown timer).
- **Format**: 45 Multiple-Choice Questions (Quant, Logic, Verbal, and CS Fundamentals).
- **Rules**:
  - Keep the test window focused; switching tabs (`TAB_BLUR`) is logged.
  - Question options are randomized to ensure fairness.
  - Unsubmitted tests auto-finalize when the timer expires.
  - Solutions and verified explanations unlock immediately after submission.

---

## 3. Understanding Your Placement Readiness Score (0–100)

Your score measures your readiness across 4 verified dimensions:

1. **Aptitude Mastery**: Speed and accuracy across quantitative, logical, and verbal topics.
2. **Technical Mastery**: Core CS concepts (Data Structures, DBMS, OS, Networks).
3. **Mock Assessment Index**: Performance in timed, proctored mock tests.
4. **Consistency**: 14-day recency and regular practice habits.

> _Statutory Notice: The Placement Readiness Score is a diagnostic preparation indicator designed to measure conceptual mastery and practice consistency. It does NOT constitute a guarantee, prediction, or legal assurance of employment or recruitment selection._

---

## 4. Following Your 28-Day Preparation Roadmap

- Access your customized roadmap at `/student/roadmap`.
- Work through daily deliberate practice tasks tailored to your weakest topics.
- Check off tasks as you finish them to boost your consistency score.
- Click **Recalibrate Roadmap** after completing new practice sets or when campus drives are scheduled.

---
