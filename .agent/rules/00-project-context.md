# 00 - Project Context & Vision

## 1. What is CampusEdge?

CampusEdge is a production-grade SaaS platform delivering AI-assisted, college-specific placement preparation and institutional intelligence for engineering colleges across India.

Unlike generic ed-tech platforms (LeetCode, HackerRank, GeeksforGeeks), CampusEdge indexes the **hyper-local placement realities** of specific campuses: historical recruitment trends, company visit patterns, branch-wise cutoffs, past interview transcripts, and college-specific evaluation criteria.

## 2. The Three Portals

1. **Student Portal**:
   - Diagnostic assessments and transparent, formula-driven readiness scores.
   - Company-specific preparation blueprints based on college hiring history.
   - Practice modules (Aptitude, Core Technical, DSA/Coding, System Design).
   - Curated senior interview experiences with verified college context.

2. **College Placement Cell (TPO Cockpit)**:
   - Centralized drive announcements, eligibility filters, and registration tracking.
   - Cohort performance diagnostics and student readiness heatmaps.
   - Review and moderation workflow for student-submitted interview experiences.
   - Historical analytics by company, package tier (Dream, Super Dream), and branch.

3. **Super Admin (Platform Governance)**:
   - College tenant provisioning, domain mapping, and institutional onboarding.
   - AI token quota tracking, model versioning, and cost controls.
   - Platform-wide telemetry, system health, and DPDP audit logs.

## 3. The Three Core Questions CampusEdge Answers

Every feature in CampusEdge must directly serve at least one of these three student-facing questions:

1. **What should I prepare?** (Topic-level priorities tailored to target companies that visit their college).
2. **Which companies should I focus on?** (Realistic fit scores based on academic criteria, past hiring history, and current readiness).
3. **How ready am I?** (Deterministic, transparent readiness score breaking down aptitude, domain skills, and coding benchmarks).
